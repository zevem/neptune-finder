# Neptune Finder interface direction

Neptune Finder shares Neptune Terminal's native visual language: one quiet
window material, content that sits in it as rounded surfaces, depth from
elevation rather than heavy borders, and a single accent reserved for focus and
selection. The explorer adapts the content and navigation structure while
retaining the exact materials, typography, icon glyphs and control geometry.

## Principles

- **Content first.** The directory and its files are the dominant surface.
  Source lists, navigation and actions support finding and acting on files.
- **Predictable navigation.** Back/forward, a parent action, breadcrumbs and an
  editable location make it clear where a pane is and how to leave it.
- **Quiet until needed.** A settled window does not repaint. Short transitions
  communicate user-triggered changes; the interface never waits for enumeration.
- **Every control is reachable and named.** Icon-only controls have accessible
  names and tooltips; editable paths and dialogs retain keyboard ownership.
- **Actions keep their target.** File operations retain the sources selected
  when the action was created and expose loading/busy and failure; success
  shows in the files themselves.

## Materials and colour

Tokens live in `src/theme.rs` (`Palette`, `metrics`). Use them rather than literal
colours. These values are copied from Neptune Terminal exactly.

| Token | Graphite | Dusk | Light | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `chrome` | `#1c1c1f` | `#1e1c2b` | `#ececef` | Toolbar and sidebar window material |
| `bg` | `#101012` | `#12111c` | `#ffffff` | Directory/file content surface |
| `elevated` | `#29292d` | `#2b2840` | `#ffffff` | Sheets, menus, floating status |
| `fg` | `#ececf1` | `#e9e7f5` | `#1d1d1f` | Primary text |
| `secondary` | `#a0a0a8` | `#a29fb8` | `#5e5e66` | Supporting text, resting icons |
| `muted` | `#6d6d76` | `#6f6c87` | `#8e8e95` | Section labels and hints |

`control`, `hover`, `pressed`, `separator` and `border` retain Terminal's
translucent white (dark themes) or black (Light) values. Floating surfaces use a
soft shadow and one-pixel border; shared materials use a separator hairline or
space. Focus/selection uses the configured accent, with its exact nine Neptune
accent choices. Red marks destructive actions. Amber marks attention. State is
always expressed with text or an icon as well as colour.

## Typography and iconography

The original bundled Geist Regular, Medium and SemiBold supply the interface.
JetBrains Mono supplies code and monospaced path/preview text. Body and buttons
are 13 points, supporting text 11, and headings 17. Weight family names and font
fallback order match Terminal. Installing bundled fonts requires no filesystem
I/O during the first frame.

Controls use Terminal's official Tabler Icons 3.49.0 subset, extended with
file-explorer glyphs from the same pinned release and regenerated by the shared
generator, with SVG sources, hashes and the MIT notice.
Use `icons::paint` and `icons::button`; do not substitute Unicode symbols, emoji,
hand-drawn paths or another icon set. The full 24-point design cell and its
optical offsets are preserved. Fractional animated bounds reuse rounded
physical-pixel rasters. Folders listed among files use Tabler's official
filled folder, so they stand apart from outline file kinds; controls,
breadcrumbs, tabs and the source list keep outline glyphs. See
[icon provenance](../assets/icons/tabler/README.md).

## Geometry and layout

Window radius is 16 points, pane radius 10 and gutter 6. Sheets use radius 14,
controls/rows 8 and menus 10. The toolbar is 44 points high, the tab strip 34
and a pane's path bar 30.
Controls are 30 points high; icon buttons retain a 28-point hit target around a
16-point glyph. Animation time remains 120 ms and tooltips wait 450 ms.

Paint the window material once; chrome layout panels stay transparent so they
cannot fill the rounded corners. Dialog scrims follow the same window shape.
As in Terminal, Windows supplies its own native border and rounding. Linux uses
square corners when maximized or fullscreen; macOS retains its painted rounding
when maximized and uses square corners in fullscreen. All four edges and all
four corners start native window resizing.

The window is composed as Terminal's is:

- **Sidebar.** Full window height, like a native source list, sharing the
  toolbar's material with no dividing line. It holds the window controls, the
  sidebar toggle at its trailing edge, then "Favorites" (Home, Desktop,
  Documents, Downloads, Pictures, Music, Videos), "Pinned" folders and
  "Locations". Rows are 30 points with a 16-point glyph and a medium 13-point
  name; the folder in view takes the `pressed` fill and an accent glyph, as a
  selected Terminal workspace does. Its footer holds "New folder" and
  Preferences. The trailing edge resizes it (170–360 points, double-click
  restores 216, saved on release). It slides in or out over 160 ms and yields
  to files below 820 points of window width; the toggle travels with it.
- **Toolbar.** 44 points, dragging the window. Back and Forward lead, then the
  folder's name in the medium weight. The search field stays centred, as
  Terminal's command field does, with its Command+F keycaps while empty, and
  trails the title in narrow windows. The trailing cluster holds "New tab",
  list and icon views and split view as toggles that stay pressed while on,
  and a "More" menu (new items, Go to Folder, hidden files, pinning, refresh,
  Preferences, About). Views and split move into that menu when room runs out.
- **Tabs.** A tab strip appears under the toolbar only with more than one tab.
  Tabs share the width equally (120–260 points); the one in view takes the
  `pressed` fill and an accent folder, and each closes from its leading corner
  under the pointer or with a middle click.
- **Panes.** Rounded `bg` surfaces inset by the gutter. With two panes, the one
  that takes the keys carries the accent ring and an accent selection; the
  other keeps its selection in a neutral fill. While searching, a strip at the
  top chooses "This folder" or "All subfolders". Every pane ends in a 30-point
  path bar: the path from Home (or the file system root) as clickable crumbs,
  collapsing from the leading end, and what the folder holds or has selected.
- **List view.** A 28-point header of Name, Date Modified, Size and Kind as
  room allows, hairline dividers, the sorted column in the foreground with its
  chevron. Dragging a divider moves it under the pointer: the column it leaves
  widens and those it moves toward give way, the nearest first. Name takes the
  remaining width and keeps at least 120 points; Date Modified (96–480), Size
  (64–240) and Kind (72–480) are saved on release, and a double click restores
  168, 78 and 150. Rows default to 28 points with faint alternating bands, a
  coloured kind glyph, relative dates ("Today at 14:02") and kinds in words
  ("PNG image", "Rust source"). Colour is decoration; the kind is always
  written.
- **Icon view.** Large glyphs over names of up to two lines. Selection backs
  the glyph with a soft tile and sets the name in an accent capsule.
- **Empty states** are a quiet glyph, a medium 14-point statement and one line
  of explanation, as Terminal says that a search found nothing.
- **Messages** sit in the window's top trailing corner only when something
  needs attention. Finished work shows in the files themselves, and a drag
  released where it began does nothing. An informational mark leaves by
  itself after four seconds; a problem keeps its red mark until dismissed.
- **Preferences.** Terminal's arrangement: a source list (General,
  Appearance, Files) concentric with the sheet beside one pane of labelled
  cards whose controls trail their rows. Themes are miniatures drawn in their
  own materials; accents are colour discs. Settings apply immediately, so
  there is no confirming action. A narrow sheet keeps the list as icons.
- **Properties.** As Ubuntu Files shows them: the item's glyph and name over
  what it holds, with a spinner while a folder is still counted, and the
  volume's free space; then Details and Permissions cards whose values trail
  their rows. Rows keep their place while properties load, and long values
  truncate with the whole value in a tooltip.

Long names truncate visibly and remain available in tooltips; long dialogs
scroll instead of clipping their actions. Quick Look titles its sheet with the
file's name and sets text on an inset content surface.

## Native visual acceptance

Inspect the running native app after the final build. Capture list, icon view,
split view, search, Quick Look, Properties and affected dialogs/preferences in
relevant themes and narrow windows. Check glyph/text alignment, density,
contrast, focus, selection, scroll behavior, truncation and overlay containment.
Screenshots of a mockup or passing widget tests do not establish native
acceptance.
